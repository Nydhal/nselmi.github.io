What does verified mean?
Saved preset: executable configuration from the explorer. Screenshot verified: sampled cell colors matched a registered screenshot. Symbolic replay verified: every decoded SVG cell matched a deterministic replay over the recorded horizon. Inferred rules and multiple possible boundaries remain explicit. Standardized preview borders may differ from the original image.
World IDs separate dynamics, geometric layout, and appearance. Changing a palette preserves the dynamics ID. Changing phase preserves dynamics but changes geometry and appearance. More rows changes the observation. Odd-width periodic worlds evolve symbolically but their alternating triangles cannot form a seamless same-row cylinder.
